<Update label="Next release" description="Make app and n8n node">
  - **Make app** and **n8n node**: the *Make a decision* module (and the *Decide* operation) lets you pick a decision of your account or **create one from the module** — from a template, from a definition, or from a one-sentence description drafted by AI. The decision is created in your account on the first run, then reused.
  - `POST /decisions` (create or reuse a decision), `GET /templates` and `GET /templates/{key}` in the REST API.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="Private preview">
  - Decisions of type **route**, **score** and **classify**, with a no-code editor, drafts, publishing and rollback.
  - **Jev** engine (TypeSafe AI) with automatic **Mistral** fallback; per-decision engine policy and per-call override.
  - **Pseudonymization** of declared fields and detected personal data before any model call.
  - Playground, test cases with *Run all*, run history with feedback, usage dashboard with quota alerts.
  - API keys, `POST /v1/decide/{slug}`, OpenAPI 3.1 specification.
  - Ten templates in English and French.
</Update>
